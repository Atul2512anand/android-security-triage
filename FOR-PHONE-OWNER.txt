FOR THE PHONE OWNER - REMOTE SECURITY SCAN
==========================================

You are allowing a consented security check of your Samsung A16. The person
checking it is on a different network, so the connection goes through
Tailscale. Nothing is installed on your phone by the scan.

You will do two things:
  A. Connect the phone to Tailscale (two possible methods - the operator
     will tell you which one).
  B. Turn on Wireless debugging and read two ports and one code to the
     operator.

Keep the phone charged and unlocked during the check.

================================================================
PART A - CONNECT THE PHONE TO TAILSCALE
================================================================

There are two ways. Use only the one the operator tells you.

----------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1 - THE OPERATOR INVITED YOUR EMAIL (easiest)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open the Tailscale invite email the operator sent you.

2. If Tailscale is not installed, install it from the Play Store:
   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tailscale.ipn

3. Open the invite link again, or open Tailscale and sign in with the EXACT
   email address the operator invited. Use the same sign-in provider that
   email uses (Google, Microsoft, or email).

4. If Tailscale asks which tailnet to join, choose the operator's tailnet.
   It is usually named after the operator, not "Personal".

5. Tap the connect button. Android asks to allow a VPN connection. Tap OK.

6. Confirm Tailscale says "Connected" and shows an address like 100.x.y.z.

7. Keep Tailscale ON. The VPN key icon must stay in the notification shade.

----------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2 - YOU KEEP YOUR OWN TAILSCALE AND SHARE ONLY THIS PHONE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Use this if you already have Tailscale or if you prefer not to join the
operator's tailnet.

1. Install Tailscale from the Play Store:
   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tailscale.ipn

2. Sign in with your own Google/Microsoft/email account.

3. If Tailscale offers to create a tailnet, accept the free personal tailnet.

4. Tap connect. Android asks to allow a VPN connection. Tap OK.

5. Confirm Tailscale says "Connected" and shows an address like 100.x.y.z.

6. Open your phone browser (Chrome or Samsung Internet) and go to:
   https://login.tailscale.com/admin/machines

7. Sign in with the same Tailscale account if asked.

8. Find THIS phone in the machine list. It should show as Android and online.
   If there are several devices, match the 100.x.y.z address shown in the
   Tailscale app.

9. Tap the "..." menu next to the phone (or the Share button).

10. Choose Share.

11. Enter the operator's Tailscale email address (the operator will give it
    to you). Confirm and send. The invite is single-use and expires after
    30 days.

12. Tell the operator to accept the share from their email.

13. Keep Tailscale ON.

IMPORTANT: Share only this phone. Do not share your whole tailnet and do not
share your other devices. If this phone is on a company/work tailnet and you
do not see a Share option, tell the operator - use Method 1 instead.

----------------------------------------------------------------
BOTH METHODS - KEEP TAILSCALE ALIVE ON SAMSUNG
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Settings > Apps > Tailscale > Battery > Unrestricted.
- Settings > Battery > Background usage limits > make sure Tailscale is not
  in "Sleeping apps" or "Deep sleeping apps".
- Turn off any other VPN (Samsung Secure Wi-Fi, a work VPN, etc.). Android
  allows only one VPN at a time.
- Wi-Fi or mobile data both work. Keep the phone online.

================================================================
PART B - TURN ON WIRELESS DEBUGGING
================================================================

1. Settings > About phone > Software information.

2. Tap "Build number" 7 times. Enter your PIN if asked.

3. Go back to Settings > Developer options.

4. Turn ON "Wireless debugging".

5. If Android asks "Allow wireless debugging on this network?", tap Allow.

6. Do not tap "Pair device with pairing code" yet. Wait for the operator to
   tell you. The port and code expire quickly.

7. When the operator says go, tap "Pair device with pairing code".

8. A popup appears with an IP address, a PORT, and a 6-digit CODE.
   Keep the popup open.

9. Read ONLY the PORT and the 6-digit CODE to the operator over the call.
   Do NOT post them in a group chat or on social media.
   The IP is not needed; the operator already knows the phone's Tailscale IP.

10. After the operator says pairing succeeded, close the popup.

11. The main "Wireless debugging" screen now shows "IP address & Port".
    Read ONLY that PORT to the operator. It is different from the pairing
    port.

12. When Android shows "Allow USB debugging?" or "Allow wireless
    debugging?", tap Allow. If "Always allow from this computer" is offered,
    tick it.

13. Optional: Developer options > turn ON "Stay awake" so the screen stays on
    while charging.

================================================================
PART C - KEEP IT READY DURING THE SCAN
================================================================

- Keep the phone unlocked and on the charger.
- Keep Tailscale connected.
- Keep Wireless debugging ON.
- Do not reboot the phone.
- Do not toggle Wireless debugging off; the ports change.
- If the pairing code expires, tap "Pair device with pairing code" again and
  read the new port and code.
- If the screen locks, unlock it.

The scan itself can take 5-15 minutes, sometimes longer on a slow connection.

================================================================
WHAT THE OPERATOR WILL DO
================================================================

- Read the installed app list and security settings.
- Collect an Android bugreport for offline analysis.
- Run a known-indicator spyware scan (MVT).
- Give you a summary and next steps.

Nothing is installed on your phone. The connection uses Android's built-in
Wireless debugging and Tailscale. You can turn both off as soon as the
operator says the scan is finished. If you used Method 2, you can also stop
sharing the phone from the Tailscale admin page.

================================================================
IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
================================================================

- Tailscale says "Disconnected": open the app, tap connect, allow the VPN
  prompt.
- The operator cannot see the phone: confirm you are signed in with the
  exact email from the invite (Method 1), or that the share was sent to the
  operator's email (Method 2).
- The phone is very warm or the battery drains: keep it on the charger;
  this is normal during a long bugreport.
- You want to stop: tell the operator. Turn off Wireless debugging and
  Tailscale. The operator will stop.
- If ADB is refused: check Settings > Security and privacy > Auto Blocker.
  If it is on, turn it off temporarily, then turn it back on after the scan.
